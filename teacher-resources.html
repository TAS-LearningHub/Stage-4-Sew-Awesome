Assessment boundary

No current assessment notification, criteria or performance descriptors are authorised in the source set. The student assessment page is historical orientation only. Schedule, template, task detail and criteria are each Teacher to confirm.

Review the assessment orientation

Before first delivery

  • Confirm delivery year, class, term and lesson allocation.
  • Confirm tote, drawstring or approved-choice pathway and the current full-size pattern.
  • Confirm the actual machine model, threading/bobbin route, local induction and pressing controls.
  • Human-hear the two optional videos or keep the complete non-video pathways.
  • Add the current approved assessment notification before describing any task as current.
